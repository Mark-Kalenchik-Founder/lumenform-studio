Hello, I’m Mark.

I’m Mark Kalenchik, the person behind Lumen&Form. I build websites for small businesses and personal brands. I prefer showing you the work to asking you to imagine it from a proposal. You’ll deal directly with me, with the scope, changes and payment terms agreed in writing.

Why show the work first?

A proposal asks you to imagine a website. A preview lets you use one.

You can open it on your phone, show it to a colleague and see whether your services, prices and contact routes make sense — before you commit to anything. It also makes feedback concrete: instead of debating a description, you point at the page and say what should change.

For One page and Business websites, you only pay once you’ve approved the final version. Extended projects are larger, so they use staged payment: 30% after homepage approval and the balance after final approval.

Working together

Collaboration is remote and in writing — email first. Scope, revisions and payment terms are agreed in writing, so decisions are easy to look back on. Enquiries receive a reply within one business day.

Preview, approval, launch.

Preview

A complete, working version of your website, hosted at a temporary address so you can explore every page. It isn’t connected to your domain yet, and forms or booking tools may still point to test settings.

Approval

You send one consolidated list of changes, I apply them, and you confirm the final version in writing. That confirmation is the point where payment becomes due.

Launch

Once payment and access are in place, I connect your domain, route the forms to your email, set up agreed integrations and hand over the files — usually within 48 hours.

An honest fit.

A good fit

  • Business websites for small companies and local services
  • Personal brands: coaches, speakers, authors, creators
  • Clear service websites with a simple route to an enquiry or booking
  • Owners who are happy to send changes in writing

Not the core offer

  • Complex web applications
  • Customer portals, dashboards and account systems
  • Sites that need daily drag-and-drop editing by the owner

If daily drag-and-drop editing is essential, a website builder may suit you better — and that’s a perfectly good choice.
