Privacy

Privacy

A short, plain description of what this website does with your information.

Enquiries

When you send the contact form, the details you enter — your name, business name, email, website link, message and any package or currency you select — are used to review and respond to your request. Nothing else is collected through the form.

Form processing

This website is hosted on Netlify, and enquiry form submissions are processed and stored by Netlify Forms so they can be delivered to the studio. Netlify’s own privacy practices apply to that processing.

No tracking, no sale

This website does not use advertising pixels or analytics tools. Enquiry data is not sold or shared for marketing.

If you choose a pricing currency, that choice is remembered in your browser for the current visit only (session storage), so the contact form can show it. It isn’t sent anywhere unless you submit the form.

Portfolio websites

The preview and concept websites linked from this site are separate websites with their own practices. Please review each site before submitting information through it.

Questions

For any privacy question, or to ask about an enquiry you have sent, email hello@lumenform.studio.
